## Interpretation rules

- The production route-protection field protects generation routes. It is not
  the answer to every sampled task. The independent search retains its own
  occupancy constraints, budget, endpoints and mesh check.
- The pilot uses explicit layouts, so random layout-screening retries are not
  present. It would be incorrect to call this a screening ablation.
- The production gate also checks construction routes and disconnected voids.
  Rejection is stricter than merely accepting one searched task. A failed grid
  search does not prove continuous-space infeasibility.
- Nominal width/height are parameters of the implicit construction. Reported
  section spans include chambers/junctions and are **local contour proxies**,
  not exact recovery of a requested corridor diameter. There is currently no
  hard tolerance contract for every realized width, turn or slope.
- Section centroid slope/turn are measured on meshes but depend on the chosen
  planes; they are not a recovered global medial axis. The pilot includes no
  realized free-space loop certification or comparison to real cave distributions.
- Canonical corrected outcomes are in `delivery_recheck.json` / `summary.json`.
  `results/*.json` preserve the original importer outcomes. No geometry was
  regenerated to fix the seam importer; checker amendment and both costs are
  disclosed. Exact preregistered code bytes are hash-verified in source_snapshots.
- Shared-workstation timings include export, checks, measurements and failed
  attempts. They are descriptive costs, not an isolated throughput benchmark.
- A C2 portal boundary overlap alarm caused a uniform 5 cm terminal-inset
  export revision. Initial portal/export attempts remain archived. This changes
  only the new C2 derivatives, not any C1 paired generation or old training asset.
